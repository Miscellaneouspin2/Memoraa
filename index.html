Frequently asked questions

What do I receive?

Delivery is defined by the selected plan, approved scope and order summary.

Can I preview before choosing?

Yes. Demo paths help compare Basic, Pro and Premium before continuing.

What is the difference between the plans?

Plans differ by structure, personalisation, gallery, RSVP, languages, revisions and after-launch support.

Are there hidden charges?

Published plan and add-on prices are references; the approved order summary must state final scope and applicable third-party costs.

What content must I provide?

Names, dates, text, photos, venues, maps and plan-specific content are collected through the workspace.

How many photos are supported?

Basic lists up to 10 customer-ready photos, Pro up to 30, and Premium limits are confirmed with the order.

Is Hindi available?

English and casual Hindi are included according to the selected plan; additional languages may be quoted separately.

How do revisions work?

Basic includes 1, Pro 2 and Premium 3 consolidated revision rounds unless the approved order states otherwise.

How long does delivery take?

The confirmed schedule appears on the order after scope and content readiness are reviewed.

Can I request urgent delivery?

The express lane is offered only after capacity and content readiness are confirmed.

Who owns the domain?

Ownership, renewal responsibility and credentials must be documented in the approved order.

Can the website be private?

Public, unlisted or protected options depend on the approved technical and commercial scope.

Will my content be used in marketing?

Only with separate and explicit portfolio consent; service delivery permission is not marketing permission.

What happens after launch?

Support duration follows the chosen plan, with renewal, care, archive, export or takedown choices handled by verified processes.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Eligibility must follow the reviewed cancellation and refund policy, payment evidence and completed production milestone.
