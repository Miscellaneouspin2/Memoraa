Privacy Policy

Data categories, purposes, consent, sharing, retention, security and customer rights.

Version 1.0Effective 20 September 2026India

Website-ready framework supplied by the owner. Professional Indian legal and accounting review is still recommended before live payments or personal-data processing.

Data we may collect

Identity/contact, order, creative content, workspace activity, technical/security, transaction references and communications.

Purposes

Enquiries, quotations, production, delivery, payment verification, authentication, security, support, legal compliance and optional purposes chosen separately.

Sharing

Only data reasonably necessary may be shared with selected service providers, advisers, authorities or a customer-authorised person. Memoraa does not sell customer personal data.

Retention

Production uploads and working assets target deletion within 2 months after final delivery when support, payment, handover and disputes are complete. A disclosed operational fallback may extend up to 6 months. Necessary contract, consent, approval, payment, grievance, security and audit records may remain longer where justified.

Rights

Subject to verification and applicable law, requests may cover access/summary, correction, completion, erasure, consent withdrawal, account deletion, grievance and nomination where available.

Security

Proportionate safeguards include HTTPS, access controls, private storage by default, validated uploads, backups, logging and environment separation. Absolute security is not promised.
